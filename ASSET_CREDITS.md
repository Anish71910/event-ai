# EventAI artwork

The six category images in `static/images/` were generated for this project using OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-18. They are fictional illustrative artwork, not photographs of actual listed events, and the interface labels them accordingly. No external stock image URLs or hotlinks are used. Generated source PNGs were encoded as 1200px WebP assets for web delivery; card images are lazy-loaded. The supplied `static/eventai-logo.jpg` is the user's original branding, retained unchanged.

Generation mode: new image; no reference photos used. Each asset was generated independently. Common direction: landscape 3:2 category artwork, sophisticated photographic detail with editorial illustration, premium travel-magazine quality, no letters, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

| Asset | Scene direction |
| --- | --- |
| music.webp | Outdoor live concert in India at dusk, silhouetted audience, violet and amber stage lights, atmospheric haze; no identifiable performer. |
| technology.webp | Intimate creative technology workshop in India, diverse adult builders with laptops at long tables, violet ambient light and cobalt screens. |
| food.webp | Indian festival tasting table with colourful small plates, chaat, spices and ceramic drinkware; warm lighting against plum colours. |
| art.webp | Adult painting workshop, close-up hands and brush, abstract canvas and paint palettes, sunlit studio with lavender and coral tones. |
| outdoors.webp | Karnataka lakeside sunrise, lush trees, mist and adult walkers seen from behind, lavender sky and warm sunrise. |
| business.webp | Founder networking on an Indian rooftop lounge, adults in small groups, warm hanging lights and a purple twilight skyline. |

Organizer-uploaded covers and galleries replace illustrative imagery where available. Uploaders should use images they own or have permission to publish. Gallery images are validated and re-encoded on the server, with EXIF orientation corrected and metadata stripped.
